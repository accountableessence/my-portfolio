✿ project one [01] FEATURED_WORK

Deha - Real-Time Yoga Pose Correction Deha - Real-Time Yoga Pose Correction

something i built that i'm actually, deeply proud of — Deha watches you move through a yoga pose in real time and gently (but firmly, don't test her) tells you when your alignment's off. built the whole thing with Python, OpenCV, and MediaPipe doing live pose estimation, wired up to Firebase for auth, session history, and profile persistence, so it remembers your practice, not just your posture. presented it at ProXpo 2026, and spent way too many hours chasing flickering skeleton overlays and mirror-flip bugs until it finally moved the way i wanted it to ✿

> PROCESS: deha.exe — real-time posture correction via pose estimation
> STACK: python / opencv / mediapipe / firebase
> pipeline reads live video feed, maps joint landmarks, flags misalignment in real time
> auth + session history + profile persistence via firestore, state survives across sessions
> showcased @ ProXpo 2026
> status: stable. still watching your form.

✿ project two [02] FEATURED_WORK

Smart Shopping List & Expense Tracker Smart Shopping List & Expense Tracker

another one i made because my wishlist dreams and my actual bank balance were not on speaking terms — a shopping list + expense tracker that lets you log what you want, what you bought, and where the money actually went, broken down by category and by trip. built with Flask on the backend, SQLite holding it all together, and Chart.js drawing the spending trends so the damage is at least pretty to look at ✿

> PROCESS: expense_tracker.py
> STACK: flask / sqlite / chart.js / html+css+js
> features: category tracking, wishlist-to-purchase flow, trip-level expense breakdown, spending trend charts
> status: stable. still watching your spending habits.
